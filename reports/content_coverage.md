# Content-Profile Coverage by History-Depth Bucket

Generated by `notebooks/content_coverage_check.ipynb`.

A user needs >=1 *described* book in their train history to get a content profile for Tier 4 at all. Book-level description coverage doesn't depend on popularity (flat ~2.6% regardless, see `reports/data_profile.md`) -- but a user's odds of ever hitting a described book scale with how many books they've read, so per-user content coverage is not flat the way per-book coverage is.

- Train items: 318,264, described items: 7,021, overlap (described AND in train): 6,842

| bucket | users with content profile | n_users | coverage |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1-2 | 457 | 19,036 | 2.4% |
| 3-5 | 662 | 9,698 | 6.8% |
| 6-20 | 1,951 | 10,562 | 18.5% |
| 21+ | 4,395 | 6,821 | 64.4% |

Implication for Tier 4: whatever content signal exists will be concentrated almost entirely in the deep-history bucket that already has the most collaborative signal to work with, and nearly absent from the sparse bucket where it would matter most.
